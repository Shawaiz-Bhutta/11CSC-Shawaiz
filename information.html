In New Zealand deforestation has reduced the country's initial forest cover which was at 80% and now down to 23%-25%. This is because of years of people igniting forests,cutting down wood for trees and making space for land.Two Major events that happened at different times cause massive deforestation. First was when Maori arrived and the second was when European people were settling down.Ways to prevent or stop deforestation include donation to save/plant trees, make stricter laws about deforestation and pest control(pest eat saplings).They could also mak specific areas to regrow native trees.Ways to prevent or stop deforestation include donation to save/plant trees, make stricter laws about deforestation and pest control(pest eat saplings).They could also mak specific areas to regrow native trees.
