In New Zealand deforestation has reduced the
country's initial forest cover which was at 80%
and now down to 23%-25%. This is because of
years of people igniting forests,cutting down
wood for trees and making space for land.
Two Major events that happened at different times cause massive deforestation. First was when Maori arrived and the second was when European people were settling down.
